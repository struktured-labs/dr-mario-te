## 6. why (sim_ramdiag.cpp + dump_lev.inc, --public-flat-rw): G2 1488 p27 preempted at 50% -> p28
- the upload area ($0500-$057F, $6124-$6127) is intact at GO; the firmware has no absolute store into it (byte scan).
- p28's first LeafEval CMD 2 (slot 1 -> CUR) is identical; at its first CMD 6 (BASE) CUR cells+links and slots 0/1 are
  IDENTICAL to the fresh run, yet the BASE takes 241 clocks instead of 1,590 and latches base_holes 0x17 instead of 0x16
  (= the stale holes_p of the preempted search); its virus walk ends at vo=0x3e instead of 0x7f. The copro reset clears
  LeafEval's st/done/modes only; ~110 other LeafEval registers (pipeline *_p, vn_hit, vo, v_r/v_c/v_col, run/span, ...)
  carry over. After a COMPLETED search they are left in a state the next BASE tolerates (wait mode: 670/670 exact);
  after a reset in the middle of a LeafEval command they are not. Same signature on a1ef31c8 (p88 -> p89: BASE 449 vs
  1,018 clocks; publishes 1 poll early, final and DONE unchanged).
