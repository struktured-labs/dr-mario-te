REFERENCE SCALE — what size effect would justify shipping?
  The #47 stranded fix moved bad-end by 22.5 points, and that IS the project's
  benchmark for 'a change worth putting on silicon'. So the question is whether
  the design can detect a #47-sized effect. At n>=40 it can (25 pts); at n=28 it
  cannot (30 pts).
