  noise LFSR) while RAM reconverges (identical f2491-f3473). At f3474 a phantom P1 RIGHT ($F5=$F7=$01, no input injected)
  appears in the flag run: the DMC-DMA / $4016 controller-read conflict of the stock ROM, landing at a different moment.
