# PREDICTION, recorded BEFORE the L11/L20 engagement counts are read

**Registered 2026-09-02T02:35Z. The L11 arm has not been built or run at the time of writing;
the L20 CvC count is still in flight and unread.**

## The mechanism's prediction

**Stranding is a TIME failure**: the capsule fails to complete a lateral trip before it locks,
so the budget depends on remaining fall time, which gravity controls.

⇒ **Faster gravity ⇒ less lateral budget ⇒ MORE stranding, and more tuck descriptors whose
budget is short ⇒ ENGAGEMENT SHOULD BE MORE COMMON AT L20 THAN AT L11.**

## Why record it

**If the counts come back the other way — engagement HIGHER at L11 than L20 — that is evidence
the mechanism is not what the adoption note says**, and it is a reason to STOP and re-derive
rather than shrug and proceed. A prediction written after the numbers cannot be surprised by
them.

## Decision rule, also fixed now

* **L11 engagement sufficient to power the paired design ⇒ RUN AT L11.** Direct relevance beats
  amplification when both are affordable, and L11 medium is the owner's actual regime — where
  "wasted a couple pills" was observed.
* **L11 engagement too sparse ⇒ RUN AT L20, labelled as a RATE AMPLIFIER**, stating that the
  absolute stranding rate does not transfer to L11 while the *direction* of the treatment effect
  should.
* ⚠ **DO NOT POOL ACROSS LEVELS.** One byte apart is still a different regime — pooling would
  repeat the mistake of treating the banked L20 farm and the L20 start-of-round population as
  one thing.
