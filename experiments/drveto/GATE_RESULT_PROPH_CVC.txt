APPLICABILITY (settled from the emitter, not assumed)
  DRPROPH is a P2-SEAT mechanism by construction: proph_trigger is JSR'd from the
  new-P2-pill edge in dispatch, and proph_pulse replaces exactly the two P2
  no-answer no-button states (the act_p2 settle guard and nf2_hold). There is no
  P1 trigger and no P1 pulse site.
  On the hardened CvC flag set P2 IS the copro champion and P1 is the DRP1NATIVE
  v28cs depth-1 6502 AI (handle(1) is dropped entirely). So the prophylactic covers
  exactly the seat whose deaths are the DRPROPH signal. It is NOT half-covered in
  the sense of a missing champion seat; the uncovered seat is the weak native AI.
